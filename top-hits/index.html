Top hits, across two corpora.

Original requests and their original assistant replies, ranked by the updated Qwen3.8-27B jailbreak-attempt probe. Blue badges identify WildChat; amber badges identify the Anomaly corpus and its underlying source.

Sexually graphic requests or replies are excluded. High scores are candidates for inspection, and can include false positives. Labels concern the request; they do not establish whether a jailbreak succeeded.

Selection. Up to 50 per corpus, highest current-probe scores after content screening, with one request per conversation and no exact repeated requests. WildChat reranks the 160 requests in the earlier published selection (originally mined from 48,009 eligible cached requests); it is not a fresh corpus-wide scan. Anomaly draws from the existing sampled usage conversations, including both the original all-turn sample and the additional-parent first-three-user-turn sample. These two selection pools differ.

Scoring. Each original user request is scored alone using Qwen3.8-27B block-32 user-end and assistant-header-end activations. Original replies are shown for inspection and are not probe inputs. Scores are raw ridge outputs, not probabilities. The reply model is the original source model, which can differ from Qwen. Any displayed English text is a machine translation; the original remains visible.

Screening. A conservative text prefilter and a full-text Luna high-reasoning review exclude graphic sexual content in either side, uncertain/failed screens, restricted biological protocols and apparent private data. Existing Anomaly human labels take precedence over saved Luna/Sol judgments; the earlier WildChat selection has not been adjudicated with the current rubric. This selected gallery estimates neither prevalence nor precision.
